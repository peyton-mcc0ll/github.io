## Project 2 (AutoCAD)
**Description:**
  - During my studies of electrical engineering I gained an interest in AutoCAD softwares and how to create layouts and designs. This interest brought me to using my educational benefits to download AutoCAD and give it a shot. I began trying to make simple designs and understanding how the software works, after that I moved onto small projects to put my understanding to the test. From these small projects I have created a simple floor plan of a house as well as a basic part design. These designs are not for any practical use, but rather for practice sake.

**Skills:**
  - Dimensioning and Annotation, 2D Drafting, Layer Management, and Understanding of Blocks

**Relevance:**
  - This project showcases foundational CAD skills used in spatial planning as well as my ability to produce accurate and organized drawings.
